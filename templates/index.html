Engineered features
Clinical indicators derived from raw vitals
Shock Index
HR / (Systolic BP + 1)
Values > 1.0 are clinically significant for sepsis risk.
Pulse Pressure
Systolic BP − Diastolic BP
Abnormal pulse pressure indicates hemodynamic instability.
Train/Test split: 80/20 stratified. StandardScaler applied before training.

Why XGBoost is the chosen model

In clinical sepsis detection, recall is more important than precision — a missed case is far more dangerous than a false alarm. XGBoost achieves 90% recall while matching Random Forest's accuracy (90%), dramatically outperforming it on recall (90% vs 73%). The tuned model with scale_pos_weight=2.5 handles class imbalance effectively, making it the most clinically appropriate model in this study.
